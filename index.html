FAQ

Do you write theses or assignments?

No. We only format and design. Your writing and research remain your own work — this keeps you safe with your university.

Which universities' formats do you follow?

Any — send us your department's guideline or a sample approved thesis and we match it exactly.

How do I pay?

Students: 50% by bKash to start, the rest on delivery. Organisations: by quotation and invoice.

Is my document kept private?

Yes. Files are used only for your order, never shared or shown as samples without your written permission.

Can you work in Bangla?

Yes — Unicode Bangla, mixed Bangla-English documents, and Bijoy (SutonnyMJ) to Unicode conversion.
